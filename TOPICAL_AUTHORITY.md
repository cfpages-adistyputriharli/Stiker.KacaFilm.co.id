## Position and boundary

The site should help homeowners, vehicle owners, designers, facility teams, and installers choose, specify, apply, inspect, clean, and replace adhesive glass films. It does not make unsupported heat, UV, privacy, safety, durability, legal-compliance, or smart-film performance claims.

## Evidence gates

- Visible-light transmission, solar/UV performance, reflectance, adhesive compatibility, glass thermal stress, smart-film electrical requirements, fire behaviour, and warranties require current manufacturer data and qualified review.
- Vehicle-film placement and darkness/reflectivity limits require current jurisdiction-specific rules; do not generalize them nationally.
- “One-way” visibility depends on relative illumination and viewing direction; it is not guaranteed at all times.
- Prices, coverage, lead times, service life, removability, and curing intervals require dated product and project evidence.
